# Owner launch checklist

Online payment must remain disabled until every item below is confirmed and the
database migration has passed staging tests.

## Business and legal

- Registered business name, registration number (if applicable), legal contact
  email, and an effective date for every policy.
- Governing law and dispute contact for the terms of sale.
- Privacy retention periods, lawful bases, processors, access/deletion contact,
  and any marketing consent process.
- Return eligibility, reporting window, return address, who pays return
  delivery, refund timing, damaged/perishable-goods rules, and cancellation rule.

## Catalogue

- Approved product names, SKUs, pack sizes, fill weights/volumes, packaging,
  retail prices, stock quantities, low-stock thresholds and shipping weights.
- Approved food specifications, ingredients/allergens where applicable, storage
  instructions, shelf life/best-before handling and required label wording.
- Approved wholesale minimums and tiers, or confirmation that wholesale remains
  quote-only.
- Final product photographs and accurate alt text. Confirm which supplied images
  may be published.

## Delivery and store

- Exact storefront/dispatch map pin for 9 Adeboale Street, Epe. The current pin
  is an approximate Epe-area pin and must not be described as doorstep-accurate.
- Confirmed pickup days, opening hours, booking lead time and collection contact.
- Service areas, excluded areas, dispatch days, delivery time ranges, weight
  bands, fees, free-delivery rules and manual-quote rules.

## Operations

- Notification email/SMTP details and the staff WhatsApp process for new orders.
- Named staff roles for catalogue publishing, inventory, order handling,
  wholesale approval and analytics access.
- Paystack test and live keys, verified webhook URL, refund process and a person
  responsible for reconciling failed/refunded sessions.
